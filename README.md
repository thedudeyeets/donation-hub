## Verified Causes & Fund Breakdown

Every single organization listed here has been independently verified with active donation portals. There are no placeholder links or corporate charity fluff.

### 🍫 Hershey & Central PA Direct Action

* **[Cocoa Packs (Hershey, PA)](https://cocoapacks.org/support/)**
  * **What the funds are for:** Delivers weekend food security packs, clothing closet essentials, and wraparound educational and emotional support for thousands of food-insecure children (preschool through age 18) across the Derry Township School District and surrounding Central Pennsylvania communities.
* **[Gemma's Angels (Hershey / Central PA)](https://www.gemmasangels.org/support-our-cause)**
  * **What the funds are for:** Operates mobile farmers' markets and food recovery operations that rescue fresh fruits, vegetables, and nutrition, delivering them directly to homeless shelters, domestic violence refuges, soup kitchens, and low-income kids in Dauphin and Lebanon counties.

### 🏛️ Harrisburg, PA Youth Programs

* **[The Joshua Group (Harrisburg, PA)](https://www.joshuagroup.org/)**
  * **What the funds are for:** Direct support for Allison Hill neighborhood youth in urban Harrisburg through free after-school academic programs, positive mentoring, literacy coaching, and scholarship/vocational pathways for at-risk students.
* **[Boys & Girls Club of Harrisburg](https://www.bgchbg.org/)**

### 🎓 Boston Youth Development & Pathways

* **[Teen Empowerment (Boston, MA)](https://teenempowerment.org/)**
  * **What the funds are for:** Employs and trains youth organizers in Roxbury and Dorchester to conduct community peace projects, violence prevention initiatives, and grassroots systemic reform campaigns across Boston.
* **[YouthBuild Boston](https://www.youthbuildboston.org/)**
  * **What the funds are for:** Provides underserved and out-of-school Boston youth with hands-on vocational trade training in sustainable construction, design, and green building trades alongside GED/academic credentialing and direct job placement.
* **[826 Boston](https://826boston.org/)**
  * **What the funds are for:** Dedicated youth writing and tutoring center supporting students ages 6–18 in Boston public schools, funding after-school creative writing workshops, college essay clinics, and student book publishing.
* **[Youth Enrichment Services (YES Boston)](https://yeskids.org/)**
  * **What the funds are for:** Outdoor equity and youth development, providing Boston urban youth with sports opportunities, ski/snowboard trips, outdoor exploration, and college readiness mentorship.

## Design System & Technical Overview

* **Design Aesthetic:** Raw Neobrutalism (Space Grotesk typography, off-white background `#f4f4f0`, thick `4px solid black` borders, and unblurred `8px 8px 0 #000` drop-shadows).
* **Interactivity:** Physical mechanical button press simulation (`translate(4px, 4px)` on hover and `translate(8px, 8px)` on active click with snappy zero-easing).
* **Vanilla JavaScript:** Fast client-side category filtering (`ALL`, `HUMANITARIAN`, `YOUTH & LOCAL`, `POLITICAL`, `ECOLOGICAL`) with real-time visible cause counts.
* **Zero Dependencies:** Pure HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JS contained in a single responsive file.
